Orchestration Final Project

Abstract

- Goal: Reorchestrate the piano score "Tales of the Old Grandmother, Op. 31, No. 1" by Sergei Prokofiev into an ensemble score
- Used MuseScore to create the ensemble score
- Extracted harmony of the piano score and split it into different instruments
- Critiqued ensemble playing the score live and gave conductor instructions to improve the performance
